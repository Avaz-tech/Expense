Privacy Policy for Xarajat

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Xarajat. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application (the "App") to track family expenses.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily enter into the App:

  • Names: Spender names you type when logging expenses.
  • Financial data: Expense amounts, categories, dates, notes, and whether an expense is personal or family.
  • Family group data: Family name and invite code if you create or join a shared family.

We do not require account registration, email, or passwords. We do not link to bank accounts or collect payment card information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Store and display your expense records on your device.
  • Optionally sync expense data with family members who join using your invite code.
  • Improve the App's functionality and user experience.

4. How We Share Your Information

  • With family members: Expense data is shared with others who join your family group using the invite code.
  • Service providers: If cloud sync is enabled, we use Supabase to store and sync data. Supabase processes this data on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you enable cloud sync, a copy is also stored on Supabase servers. You can delete local data by leaving your family group or uninstalling the App.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

You may delete your expense data at any time within the App. To request deletion of cloud-synced data, contact us using the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us
